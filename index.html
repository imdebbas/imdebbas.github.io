GrokBot Tasks — Privacy Policy

GrokBot Tasks is a personal tool used only by its owner, Max Debbas, to read his own Google Tasks.

What it accesses: read-only access to the signed-in user's Google Tasks (scope: tasks.readonly). It cannot create, change, or delete tasks.

How data is used: task titles and due dates are read on the owner's own computer to create personal reminders. Data is not sold, shared, or sent to any third party, and is not used for advertising.

Storage: the sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. No task data is kept on any server.

Revoking access: remove "GrokBot Tasks" at https://myaccount.google.com/connections at any time.

Contact: imdebbas@gmail.com
